Rank 1, round 3, seed Communications researcher

The last comment before a restatement

QuestionIn a discussion of about five people, is the comment immediately before a person restates a view more associated with the direction of that restatement than the first disagreeing comment?

ClaimPeople answer the latest turn, not the strongest objection, when the group is small enough that every comment stays on screen.

What changedThe method now ends the thread after the assigned comments, and it adds a private scale item that uses the same endpoints as the public statement. The outcome names both.

Rank 2, round 1, seed Social psychologist

Peer dissent in a five-person thread

QuestionWhen someone has just stated an opinion, does one disagreeing comment from another member of the same small group shift the next stated opinion more than one disagreeing comment from someone outside the group?

ClaimDissent from inside the group is harder to set aside as ignorance of the group's norms, so the update should be larger.

What changedThe revision adds a private scale item after the thread closes, because the critic said a public concession was not an update.

Rank 3, round 5, seed Political scientist

Party label versus a peer with no label

QuestionDoes a comment that names a party move a stated opinion more than a comment with the same argument and no party name, in a group of five strangers?

ClaimIn a group of strangers, a party label is a shortcut, and it should move the restatement more than an unlabeled argument of the same length.

What changedThe revision matches the labeled and unlabeled comments on length and on the argument, because the critic said a longer comment could imitate a party cue.

Rank 4, round 2, seed Computer scientist

How far a restatement moves from the prior sentence

QuestionIn public comment threads of about five speakers, how far does a person's restated opinion move, in embedding distance, after the intervening comments?

ClaimDistance from the original sentence tracks an update even when the person never writes an explicit change of mind.

What changedThe revision drops threads shorter than five speakers before scoring, because the critic said the distance was not comparable across thread lengths.

Rank 5, round 4, seed Sociologist

Who is allowed to change their mind

QuestionIn a small online group, do speakers with fewer ties to the others update a stated opinion more often than speakers with more ties?

ClaimDense ties turn a stated opinion into a commitment, so the well-connected speaker updates less.

What changedThe revision drops threads where one person wrote most of the replies, because degree in those threads is not a tie so much as a volume effect.
