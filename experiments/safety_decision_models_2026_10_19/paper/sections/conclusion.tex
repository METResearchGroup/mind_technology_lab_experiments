\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Teams choosing a screen should publish the guardrail crosswalk and report miss, FPR, and cost on the same rows rather than a single pooled headline.
JailbreakBench shows that guardrail headline miss under a Prompt-Attack-only crosswalk is largely a mapping artifact; post-hoc any-filter aggregation and decision models at 0.5 sit elsewhere on the same rows.
HarmBench and Do-Not-Answer aggregates hide opposing strengths by attack type and risk area.
Stacks add value where the guardrail still blocks harm and benign FPR stays low; they collapse when the guardrail path is already saturated.
Artifacts for runs \texttt{20261020T033822Z\_0f24960e} and \texttt{20261020T040920Z\_90c5fd13} support reproduction without quoting harmful prompts.
