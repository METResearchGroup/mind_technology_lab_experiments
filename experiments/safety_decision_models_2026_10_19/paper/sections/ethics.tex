\section{Ethics and Broader Impact}
\label{sec:ethics}

The benchmarks include hate, harassment, sexual content, self-harm themes, and jailbreaks.
We do not quote those prompts, and we do not reproduce Do-Not-Answer question text in the paper, the repository, or the S3 release.
Do-Not-Answer is used under its academic-use terms.
Risk-area rates use the dataset's own category names.
A miss means the screen allowed the text under the stated threshold and mapping.
It is not evidence that a chat model complied, and it is not a measure of harm to a person.
A false positive can block ordinary emotional text.
The comparison is a configuration result on fixed public prompts, not a certification of a deployed product.
