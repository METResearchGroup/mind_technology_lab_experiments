\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Small test sets (prompt injections $n=116$; JailbreakBench 100 harmful / 100 benign) widen uncertainty.
JailbreakBench thresholds stay at 0.5 because the labeled development pool is under 200 rows.
WildGuardMix tuning can lower test F1 relative to 0.5 when benign development labels are sparse.
Clef may truncate over-long inputs; we do not isolate truncation effects.
Guardrail bands are ordinal, not probabilities.
GoEmotions guardrail miss 1.0 reflects an empty crosswalk (all rows allowed), not universal API blocking.
We score fixed prompts in \texttt{us-east-2} at 2026-10-19 list prices; results need not transfer to other regions, traffic, or reserved pricing.
