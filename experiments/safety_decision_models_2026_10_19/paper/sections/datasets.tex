\section{Datasets}
\label{sec:datasets}

Eleven Hugging Face datasets supply prompt-level labels: nine safety or jailbreak corpora plus GoEmotions and Emotion~\cite{hartvigsen2022toxigen,markov2023holistic,lin2023toxicchat,deepset2024promptinjections,han2024wildguard,wang2024donotanswer,rottger2024xstest,mazeika2024harmbench,chao2024jailbreakbench,demszky2020goemotions,saravia2018carer}.
We score user text only, never model replies.
Development pools fit thresholds (maximize harmful-class F1 with benign FPR $\leq 5\%$ on labeled benign rows, else 0.5); JailbreakBench stays at 0.5 because its labeled development pool has fewer than 200 rows.
HarmBench merges DirectRequest ($n=320$ test) and HumanJailbreaks ($n=1600$ test) for overall miss.
JailbreakBench balances 100 harmful and 100 benign goals.
Do-Not-Answer reports detection rate and risk-area aggregates without reproducing item text.
GoEmotions and Emotion use empty guardrail crosswalks (miss rate 1.0 means all rows pass the guardrail layer under our block definition).
Full row counts and split rules are in the appendix.
