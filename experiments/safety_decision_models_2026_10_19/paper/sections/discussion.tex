\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Publish the guardrail crosswalk with any headline miss rate: JailbreakBench shows that Prompt-Attack-only mapping dominates strength sweeps.
Do not compare a single miss number across harm types when HarmBench configs and Do-Not-Answer risk areas pull in opposite directions.
Use union when the guardrail still catches harmful rows and its benign FPR is low (XSTest); expect little from cascade when the deferral band is a single point on WildGuardMix.
Stacking cannot rescue a guardrail that already misses 0.98 of harmful JailbreakBench rows under the registered mapping.
Jev wins on latency and price in this pack but not on every miss metric.

These rules describe prompt-level filters on public benchmarks.
They are not a certification of a mental-health product, and they do not test users or downstream chat models.
Miss means the screen allowed the text under stated thresholds; it is not attack success against a target LLM.
